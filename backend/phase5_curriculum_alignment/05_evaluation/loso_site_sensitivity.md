## Skill-level stability (what actually moved)

Pooled over all 32 holdout × cohort comparisons:

- **0 status changes.** No skill present in both top-30 lists was ever judged covered with a portal in and not covered with it out. Removing a portal does not re-score a skill; the coverage decision for a given skill is independent of which portal supplied the postings.
- **Demand ranking holds**: Spearman-style rank correlation on the shared skills, median 0.96, worst case 0.84.
- **What churns is the tail of the cut-off.** The skill at rank 30 is mentioned in only 16 of 254 postings, so top-30 membership at the boundary is decided by a handful of adverts. Every covered-count movement below traces to substitutions at that boundary, not to a changed score.
